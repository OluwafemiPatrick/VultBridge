VultBridge third-party notices
==============================

The release build replaces this inventory with the exact runtime artifact filenames and copies
the applicable license and notice resources from those resolved JARs into `licenses/`. The two
licenses that are not shipped as resources by their runtime JARs are committed alongside this
file:

- `licenses/OpenJFX-GPLv2-with-Classpath-Exception.txt`
- `licenses/Bouncy-Castle-License.txt`

The runtime dependency versions and their legal-source mapping are recorded in
`docs/release/dependency-licenses.md`. A release is incomplete unless this notice and the complete
`licenses/` directory are present in the application image.
